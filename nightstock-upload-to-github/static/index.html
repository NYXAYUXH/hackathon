🎤 3-Minute Hackathon Presentation Cheatsheet

⏱️ Minute 0:00 - 1:00 (Speaker 1 - The Hook & Problem)

"Hello judges, we are Team NIGHTSTOCK. In modern retail and supply chains, stockouts lead to lost revenue while overstocking leads to tied-up capital and spoilage. We built NIGHTSTOCK — The Inventory of the Damned — combining smart inventory controls with machine-learning demand prediction in a thrilling Halloween theme."

⏱️ Minute 1:00 - 2:00 (Speaker 2 - Live Operations)

"Here is our live dashboard. We track inventory valuation, stockout warnings, and health index. Let me show you our Inventory Crypt: we can filter items, do instant stock updates, or log ritual consumption. Notice how everything updates in real time with zero latency."

⏱️ Minute 2:00 - 3:00 (Speaker 3 - AI Demand Engine & Spikes)

"Now for our core innovation: Demand Prediction. Using historical consumption, our Scikit-Learn Ridge Regression model forecasts demand for the next 7 days with 95% confidence intervals. It calculates recommended reorder quantities and explains in plain English why restocking is needed. Watch as I inject a sudden surge spike: our engine flags the anomaly with a Z-score calculation and instantly generates a warning alert!"
